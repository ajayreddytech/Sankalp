# SANKALP

**Commit to what matters, one day at a time.**

Most habit apps ask one question: did you do it, yes or no. SANKALP asks the right question for each kind of commitment, and it keeps an honest record of every attempt, including the ones that didn't work out.

## The problem

Most people don't fail because they lack willpower. They fail because a single missed day feels like the end, the record disappears, and there's no clear way to see progress or pick up again.

## The idea

SANKALP treats commitment as a journey, not a streak:

- **Three kinds of commitment, each with its own rules.**
  - **Habit:** 66 days, the length research commonly links to a habit taking hold. No exceptions.
  - **Sankalpa:** a vow for the number of days you choose, with no exceptions.
  - **SMART goal:** a plan of measurable actions, logged daily toward a deadline.
- **Setbacks are recorded, not erased.** Every attempt stays in your history, so you can see how far you have come and start again from a clear point.
- **Your reason comes first.** Every journey asks *why*, and that is what you see on the hard days.

## What it does

- **Check In:** everything due today on one screen. Tap Done or Missed.
- **Journey grid:** a visible map of your days, from done to still ahead.
- **SMART tracking:** log your numbers each day, see what remains, what you need from here on, and where you are ahead or behind.
- **Accomplishments:** every finished journey, every miss, and every deleted one, kept in one place.
- **Redo:** start an accomplished journey again with a new date, with its record kept intact.
- **Reorder:** arrange your journeys once, and the order is the same on every device.
- **Works like an app:** installs to your phone or desktop and opens instantly, even without a connection. Your journeys sync when you're back online.

## Built to be trusted

- **Your data is yours.** Everything lives in your own Google account, behind security rules that let only you read or write it.
- **No hidden copies.** The app keeps nothing in the browser's local storage.
- **Export anytime.** Download all your data as a JSON backup whenever you want.
- **Safe on two devices.** Each check-in is saved as one transaction, so the same day can't be counted twice.

## Why it can change a life

Small, kept promises build self-trust. A person who can see seventy days of honest progress, with the misses included, learns that they can come back after a setback. That is the skill that carries over into health, work, learning and relationships.
